# Growth Branch: Technical Overlay

## Investment Context

This analysis is for a **growth portfolio**. Favor momentum and trend continuation. Growth stocks trend strongly — emphasize momentum over mean-reversion.

## How This Changes Your Analysis

When you work through the base framework, adjust your interpretation:

1. **Trend (step 1)**: Bullish SMA alignment is CRITICAL for growth. Price above all SMAs = growth stock in its sweet spot. Price below SMA 200 = institutional abandonment, which is a strong negative for growth names.
2. **Momentum (step 2)**: RSI above 60 is NORMAL for trending growth stocks. Do not penalize overbought readings — strong growth trends sustain RSI 60-80 for months. RSI below 40 is unusual and negative for growth stocks.
3. **Volume (step 3)**: Above-average Volume Ratio during advances confirms institutional accumulation — very bullish for growth.
4. **S/R (step 4)**: Breakout above Resistance 1 on above-average volume = the strongest growth buy signal.
5. **Returns (step 5)**: All positive returns across all timeframes (1W through 12M) = sustained institutional demand. This is HIGH priority for growth.

## Score Modifiers

Apply these after your base analysis:

- Current Price above Resistance 1 with Volume Ratio above 1.1x (breakout): **+1**
- All returns positive (1W, 1M, 3M, 6M): **+1**
- MACD Histogram expanding with price above SMA 50: **+1**
- RSI (14) below 40: **-1** (growth stocks should have positive momentum)
- Price below SMA 200: **-1** (institutional abandonment)
