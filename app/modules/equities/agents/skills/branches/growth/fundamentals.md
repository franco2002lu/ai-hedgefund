# Growth Branch: Fundamentals Overlay

## Investment Context

This analysis is for a **growth portfolio**. Prioritize companies with accelerating revenue, expanding markets, and competitive positioning. Profitability can be secondary to growth trajectory.

## How This Changes Your Analysis

When you work through the base framework, adjust your priorities:

1. **Valuation (step 1)**: De-emphasize P/E. A P/E of 40+ is acceptable if PEG Ratio is below 1.5. Focus on PEG as the primary valuation metric. PEG below 1.0 = strong buy signal.
2. **Profitability (step 2)**: Focus on Gross Margin over Net Margin. Gross Margin above 50% signals pricing power and scalability. ROE may be low or negative for reinvesting growth companies — this is acceptable.
3. **Growth (step 3)**: This is your HIGHEST priority step. Revenue Growth (YoY) above 20% is the key threshold. Earnings Growth exceeding Revenue Growth = margin expansion while growing = ideal. Revenue Growth below 10% is a penalty — this is a growth portfolio.
4. **Cash flow (step 5)**: Negative FCF is acceptable if Revenue Growth is accelerating. But check Current Ratio for cash runway — below 1.0 with negative FCF is dangerous.

## Score Modifiers

Apply these after your base analysis:

- Revenue Growth (YoY) above +30%: **+1**
- Earnings Growth exceeding Revenue Growth (margin expansion): **+1**
- PEG Ratio below 1.0: **+1**
- Gross Margin above 50% with Revenue Growth above 15%: **+1**
- Revenue Growth (YoY) below +10%: **-1** (growth portfolio penalty)
- Revenue Growth decelerating for 2+ quarters (check Recent Earnings trend): **-2** (major growth red flag)
- Negative FCF with Current Ratio below 1.0: **-1** (cash runway risk)
