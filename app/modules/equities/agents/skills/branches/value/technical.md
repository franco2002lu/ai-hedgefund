# Value Branch: Technical Overlay

## Investment Context

This analysis is for a **value portfolio**. Favor mean-reversion and recovery signals. Value stocks are often out of favor — look for bottoming, accumulation, and trend reversal rather than momentum continuation.

## How This Changes Your Analysis

When you work through the base framework, adjust your interpretation:

1. **Trend (step 1)**: Price below SMAs is EXPECTED for value stocks. Do not automatically score this bearish. What matters is whether the decline is stabilizing. SMA 20 flattening or turning up while below SMA 50 = early recovery signal.
2. **Momentum (step 2)**: RSI (14) below 30 near Support 1 is the CLASSIC value entry signal — this is BULLISH in a value context, not bearish. MACD bullish crossover after an extended downtrend = potential trend reversal.
3. **Volume (step 3)**: Volume Ratio increasing after a prolonged decline = institutional accumulation. This is one of the earliest re-rating signals and is HIGH priority.
4. **S/R (step 4)**: Price near Support 1 with stabilizing volume = favorable risk/reward for value buyers. HIGH priority.
5. **Returns (step 5)**: Negative long-term returns are expected. What matters is whether short-term returns (1W, 1M) are improving relative to long-term (6M, 12M) — this signals a turning point.

## Score Modifiers

Apply these after your base analysis:

- RSI (14) below 30 with price near Support 1: **+1** (oversold opportunity)
- Volume Ratio increasing after 3M+ of negative returns (accumulation): **+1**
- MACD bullish crossover (Histogram turning positive) after extended downtrend: **+1**
- Price below SMA 200 but short-term returns improving (1W or 1M positive): **+1** (reversal signal)
- Accelerating decline: all returns negative AND getting worse at shorter timeframes: **-1** (falling knife)
