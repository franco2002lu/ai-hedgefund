# Value Branch: News Overlay

## Investment Context

This analysis is for a **value portfolio**. Seek re-rating catalysts. The most powerful value catalysts are insider buying, activist involvement, and events that force the market to re-evaluate an undervalued company.

## How This Changes Your Analysis

When classifying and weighting headlines, adjust your priorities:

1. **Insider buying** (step 4): Treat as HIGHEST impact — stronger than earnings for value stocks. CEO/CFO purchasing shares with personal funds is the strongest conviction signal. Insiders have the most information about whether the stock is truly undervalued or a value trap.
2. **Activist involvement**: Treat as HIGH impact. Activist investors taking positions or pushing for changes (spinoffs, buybacks, board seats) are powerful re-rating catalysts.
3. **Dividend increases**: Treat as HIGH impact. A dividend raise signals management confidence in future cash flows — directly relevant to value investors.
4. **Share buybacks**: Treat as MEDIUM-HIGH impact. Buybacks at depressed valuations are accretive and signal management believes the stock is undervalued.
5. **Analyst re-ratings**: Treat as HIGH impact. Upgrades from sell/underperform to neutral/buy trigger significant moves in neglected value stocks.
6. **Product launches / growth narrative**: Treat as LOW impact. Growth-oriented news is less relevant for value investors focused on cash flow and margin of safety.

## Score Modifiers

Apply these after your base analysis:

- Insider buying (CEO/CFO personal purchases) in Last 7 Days or Last 30 Days: **+2**
- Activist investor takes significant position: **+1**
- Dividend increase announced: **+1**
- Share buyback program announced at depressed valuation: **+1**
- Analyst upgrade from sell/underperform to neutral or better: **+1**
- Dividend cut announced: **-2** (destroys the value thesis)
- Headlines suggesting structural decline in core business: **-2**
