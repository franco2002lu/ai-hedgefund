# Value Branch: Fundamentals Overlay

## Investment Context

This analysis is for a **value portfolio**. Seek undervalued companies with strong cash generation, sustainable dividends, and catalysts for re-rating. Focus on margin of safety and downside protection.

## How This Changes Your Analysis

When you work through the base framework, adjust your priorities:

1. **Valuation (step 1)**: This is your HIGHEST priority step. P/E (TTM) below 15 for a profitable large-cap is attractive. P/E below the sector average is a value signal. Also check P/B — below 1.5 suggests the market undervalues the assets.
2. **Profitability (step 2)**: Focus on ROE — consistently above 12% shows the business generates good returns. Declining ROE is a value trap warning. Net Margin stability matters more than expansion.
3. **Growth (step 3)**: MEDIUM priority. Value stocks don't need high growth, but Revenue Growth should be stable or positive (above 0%). Revenue decline for 2+ periods is a value trap signal.
4. **Balance sheet (step 4)**: HIGH priority for value. Debt/Equity below 0.8 provides margin of safety. Current Ratio above 1.5 means the company can weather downturns. This is about downside protection.
5. **Cash flow (step 5)**: HIGHEST priority alongside valuation. FCF Yield above 5% is a strong buy signal. Above 8% is exceptional. Compare Dividend Yield to FCF Yield — if Dividend Yield exceeds FCF Yield, the dividend is at risk.

## Score Modifiers

Apply these after your base analysis:

- P/E (TTM) below 12 with positive FCF: **+1**
- FCF Yield above 8%: **+1**
- Dividend Yield above 3% with sustainable payout (Dividend Yield < FCF Yield): **+1**
- ROE above 15% with stable or growing Revenue: **+1**
- ROE declining for 2+ consecutive periods: **-2** (value trap warning)
- Revenue Growth negative for 2+ periods: **-1** (structural decline risk)
- Dividend Yield exceeding FCF Yield: **-1** (dividend sustainability risk)
- Debt/Equity above 1.5 with flat or negative Revenue Growth: **-1**
